## 摘要

The Taiwan EPA (Environmental Protection Administration) successfully finished air and water sampling in Taiping (Itu-Aba) Island, Nansha(Spratly) Islands and the nearby sea area from September 21~27, 2009. The results of sampling analysis indicate both the air quality and the water quality in the area being in good condition. This provides crucial information about environmental conditions in the southern-most area of the country for the first time.
